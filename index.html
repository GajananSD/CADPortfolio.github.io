// Who am I

About

I completed my B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering, with a minor in Computer Science & Engineering, at IIT Gandhinagar. This site is a set of drawing sheets from that academics: CAD models spanning mechanisms, controls and robotics, thermal experiments, powertrain components, and product design, built in Fusion 360, Inventor, and SolidWorks.

Every project below started the same way every real design does — as a sketch, a set of constraints, and a problem worth solving properly.
